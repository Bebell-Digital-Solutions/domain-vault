## ⚡ Why DOMAIN VAULT?

Replace messy spreadsheets with a high-performance system designed for domain investors, developers, and agencies.[cite: 6] **DOMAIN VAULT** never asks for registrar credentials and never scrapes your accounts.[cite: 12] Add a domain, hit the WHOIS button to pull the creation and expiry dates, set your costs, and it's tracked.[cite: 12] 

No API keys. No OAuth. Just your data, securely in one place.[cite: 12]

## 🚀 Key Features

* **📊 All-in-One Dashboard:** View your entire domain portfolio at a glance, including total domains, annual costs, upcoming renewals, and provider distribution.[cite: 3]
* **📅 Automated Renewals Calendar:** Sync expiration dates directly to Google Calendar or download `.ics` files.[cite: 6] Get WhatsApp reminders before you lose valuable digital real estate.[cite: 1, 6]
* **🏢 Multi-Provider Vault:** Store secure credentials for GoDaddy, Namecheap, Cloudflare, IONOS, and custom registrars in one encrypted vault.[cite: 3, 6]
* **🔐 Zero-Knowledge Security:** Your vault lives in a Supabase Postgres database protected by Row-Level Security (RLS) hosted on AWS, meaning only you can read your rows.[cite: 5, 10, 12] Credential storage uses zero-knowledge AES-256 client-side encryption.[cite: 4, 10]
* **🌐 Live DNS Inspector:** Query live DNS records (A, TXT, MX, CNAME) directly from the dashboard to verify propagation and structural integrity without opening a terminal.[cite: 6]
* **📈 Financial Reporting:** Track renewal costs by month, visualize your true domain investment, and export comprehensive CSV reports to hand off to your accounting team.[cite: 3, 6]
* **🌍 Bilingual UI:** Seamlessly switch between English and Español with one click.[cite: 5]

## 💡 How It Works (Zero-Config Workflow)

1. **Add Domains:** Manually add your domains or use the **Auto-fill using WHOIS** tool to instantly fetch registration and expiry dates.[cite: 1, 12]
2. **Set Costs:** You decide what counts as a purchase and an annual renewal cost.[cite: 12]
3. **Monitor:** Use the dashboard to get warned about upcoming expirations (e.g., "Expiring in 30 Days") and DNS errors.[cite: 1, 6]
